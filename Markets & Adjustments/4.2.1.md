# Section 4.2.1 – Market, Price System and Invisible Hand

This section explains how buyers and sellers interact in a market and how prices are determined through demand and supply.

## 1. What is a market?

In economics, a market is not necessarily a physical place.

It is an arrangement where buyers and sellers exchange goods and services.

Examples: Vegetable markets, labour markets and money markets.

A market requires:

- Demand from buyers.
- Supply from sellers.
- A medium of exchange, generally money.

## 2. What is a price system?

The price system is a mechanism through which demand and supply determine the prices of goods and services.

For example:

- High demand and low supply generally increase prices.
- Low demand and high supply generally decrease prices.

## 5. How does the price mechanism adjust?

There are two situations:

A. Excess supply

When supply is greater than demand:

- Sellers have unsold goods.
- Market price decreases.
- Demand increases and supply decreases.
- Eventually, the market moves towards equilibrium.

B. Excess demand

When demand is greater than supply:

- Buyers compete for limited goods.
- Market price increases.
- Demand decreases and supply increases.
- Eventually, the market moves towards equilibrium.

## 6. Price mechanism as an invisible hand

Important concept

The economist Adam Smith described the price mechanism as an Invisible Hand.

It means that individuals make independent economic decisions to satisfy their own interests, but their decisions are coordinated through market prices.

Example:

Suppose the demand for rice increases.

1. Rice prices increase.
2. Farmers receive a signal that rice production can be more profitable.
3. They are encouraged to produce more rice.
4. More resources move towards rice production.

Thus, without anyone directly instructing farmers, market prices influence their decisions.

This is called the Invisible Hand.

### Quick revision

| Concept               | Meaning                                     |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Market                | Arrangement between buyers and sellers      |
| Price system          | Demand and supply determine prices          |
| Market-clearing price | Price where demand equals supply            |
| Excess supply         | Supply exceeds demand                       |
| Excess demand         | Demand exceeds supply                       |
| Invisible Hand        | Prices guide independent economic decisions |

Key takeaway: The price mechanism coordinates the decisions of buyers and sellers and helps allocate society's available resources.
